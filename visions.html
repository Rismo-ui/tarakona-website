No-AI Art

While minimal AI art was used in the prototyping of the app and foundational pieces, Tarakona stands firmly for artists and against incorporating AI into human creativity. To this end, each app will feature and shout out any artists used in the development, whether featured presently or in the past.

We pledge to never make our apps worse to make more money. No dark patterns, no degrading the free experience just to push upgrades. If we ever break that promise, hold us to it.
