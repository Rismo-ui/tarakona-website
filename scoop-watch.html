Scoop Watch

A litter box reminder that doesn't let you swipe it away and forget. Clearing it takes a few real seconds of attention — so the box actually gets cleaned, not just dismissed.

Notify me at launch See the roadmap
The problem

Reminders are easy to ignore. That's the whole problem.

A notification you can clear with one swipe gets cleared with one swipe — whether or not the box actually got scooped. Scoop Watch works differently: dismissing a reminder means actually doing something first, so "I'll get to it later" stops being an option that costs nothing.

How it works

Three things that make it stick

01

A puzzle stands between you and "later"

Dismissing a reminder means completing a short pet-themed mini-game first. It's quick, but it's not nothing — and if the box still isn't clean, the reminder comes right back.

02

Every cat, every box, on its own schedule

Track multiple litter boxes and cats independently, with interval or scheduled cleaning times and a streak that rewards actually keeping up.

03

Quiet when you need it to be

Discreet notification styles and quiet hours mean Scoop Watch fits into your day instead of interrupting it — persistent, not obnoxious.
