Scoop Watch Support

Help for the Scoop Watch app

Scoop Watch is made by one person with some help from friends. If something isn't working, or you have an idea, we want to hear about it. We read every message.

Contact us

Email support@tarakonaindustries.com. To help us help you, please include:

  • What went wrong, or what you would like changed;
  • What you expected to happen;
  • The steps that make it happen;
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version;
  • The app version, shown at the bottom of the Behind the Scenes screen in the app;
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if you can.

For fixes and ideas, a subject line like "Fix: a short title" is perfect.

Common questions

My reminders aren't arriving.

Scoop Watch reminders are scheduled on your iPhone and delivered by iOS, so a few phone settings can get in the way:

  • In the iPhone Settings app, open Notifications, then Scoop Watch, and make sure notifications are allowed;
  • Check whether a Focus or Do Not Disturb mode is on;
  • Low Power Mode can delay notifications;
  • Open Scoop Watch once. The app refreshes its reminders whenever it opens;
  • iOS limits how many reminders an app can have scheduled at once, so with several litter boxes Scoop Watch may space reminders further apart.

What happens when I tap a reminder?

Tapping a reminder opens the Home screen. Any box that needs cleaning is shown in red. Tap a box to start the quick puzzle that marks it clean. Reminders keep coming until the box is marked clean.

How do I restore my Scoop Watch+ subscription?

Open the Profile tab, tap Subscriptions, then tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel Scoop Watch+?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions, and choose Scoop Watch+. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. If you cancel, the free app keeps working, and your saved choices come back if you subscribe again.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refund requests go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

A photo isn't showing.

Make sure you're on the latest version of Scoop Watch, then try choosing the photo again. If it still doesn't appear, email us with your iPhone model and iOS version.

What data does Scoop Watch collect?

There are no accounts, and your boxes, pets, photos, and history stay on your phone. The one exception is Scoop Watch+, where a subscription service receives limited purchase information to verify and restore your subscription. The details are in our Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete individual pets and litter boxes inside the app, or delete all of Scoop Watch's data by deleting the app from your iPhone.
