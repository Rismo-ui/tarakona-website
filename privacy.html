Privacy Policy

Scoop Watch · Effective Date: November 1, 2026

Scoop Watch is developed and operated by Tarakona Industries LLC ("Tarakona Industries," "we," "us," or "our"), a South Carolina limited liability company. This Privacy Policy explains what information Scoop Watch collects, how it is used, and your choices regarding that information.

1. Summary

Scoop Watch is designed to work on your device. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not operate a server that stores your personal information. The information you enter into the app, such as your name, your pets' details, and your reminder preferences, stays on your device unless you choose to contact us directly (for example, by emailing support).

The one exception is optional: if you subscribe to Scoop Watch+, a subscription-management service called RevenueCat receives limited purchase information so that your subscription can be verified and restored. Section 4 explains exactly what that is.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

When you use Scoop Watch, the following information is stored locally on your device only:

  • Your name and date of birth, as entered during setup;
  • Information about your pet(s), including name, breed, coat pattern, and any photo you choose to add as an emblem or pet photo;
  • Litter box information, cleaning schedules, and cleaning history (including streaks);
  • App preferences, such as notification style, quiet hours, background/theme selections, and animation settings;
  • Subscription status for Scoop Watch+ (and any future subscription), as reported to the app by Apple and by our subscription provider (see Section 4), together with a randomly generated anonymous identifier used for that purpose.

This information is not transmitted to Tarakona Industries, is not stored on any server we operate, and is not shared with any third party by us. It remains on your device and, if you have device backups enabled (such as iCloud Backup), may be included in those backups as part of your device's own backup process, which is managed by Apple and governed by Apple's own privacy practices, not ours.

3. Photos

If you choose to add a photo (for example, as a pet emblem or profile picture), Scoop Watch accesses your device's photo library only with your permission, granted through your device's operating system. The selected photo is stored locally on your device as part of the app's data and is not uploaded to us or any third party.

4. Subscriptions and Payments

Scoop Watch+ is an optional subscription. It is billed and processed entirely by Apple through the App Store's in-app purchase system. We do not receive, process, or store your payment card information, billing address, or any other payment details. Apple handles all of that directly under Apple's own privacy policy.

To verify, restore, and keep track of subscriptions, Scoop Watch uses RevenueCat, a third-party subscription-management service that acts as our service provider. If you use Scoop Watch+ or tap Restore Purchases, the following information is sent from your device to RevenueCat:

  • A randomly generated anonymous identifier created on your device. It is not linked to your name, email address, or Apple ID, and we do not ask you to create an account;
  • Purchase and subscription information provided by Apple, such as the product, purchase and expiration dates, and subscription status;
  • Technical information needed to deliver the service, such as your device's IP address, app and operating system versions, and your App Store country or region.

RevenueCat does not receive your name, date of birth, photos, pet information, or other content you enter into Scoop Watch. We use this information to determine whether your subscription is active so the app can unlock the appropriate features, to help prevent fraud, and to understand overall subscription activity, such as how many people subscribe. RevenueCat's handling of this information is described in its own privacy policy. We do not sell this information and do not use it for advertising.

5. Notifications

Scoop Watch's cleaning reminders are scheduled and delivered locally on your device using your device's operating system. We do not operate a push-notification server, and reminder content is not transmitted off of your device.

6. Contacting Us and Support Links

If you choose to contact us directly — for example, through a support or feedback email link within the app — we will receive whatever information you choose to include in that message, such as your email address and the content of your message. We use this information solely to respond to you and to improve Scoop Watch, and we do not use it for advertising or share it with third parties. You may ask us to delete any correspondence you've sent us at any time by contacting us using the information in Section 10.

The App includes an optional link to a support page on Buy Me a Coffee (buymeacoffee.com), a service run by a third party, where you can choose to make a voluntary donation. Tapping the link opens that page in your device's web browser. The App does not send any information about you to that service, and what happens on that page, including any information you provide there, is governed by the service's own terms and privacy policy, not this one. We do not receive your payment details, and we do not require a donation to use the App.

7. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Apart from the subscription service described in Section 4, Scoop Watch does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting tools, and does not track your activity across other companies' apps or websites. If this changes in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy and the App Store's App Privacy disclosure to accurately reflect any new data collection before it takes effect.

8. Children's Privacy

Scoop Watch is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because the app does not collect information on our servers, we do not maintain any of your information for any user, including children, in a location we control. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information through a support or contact email, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. Data Deletion and Your Choices

Because your information is stored locally on your device, you are in control of it at all times:

  • You may delete individual pets, boxes, or other entries directly within the app, where such options are provided;
  • You may delete all app data by uninstalling Scoop Watch from your device;
  • If you have contacted us directly by email, you may request that we delete that correspondence by contacting us using the information in Section 10;
  • If you would like purchase-related information held by our subscription provider deleted, contact us using the information in Section 10 and we will work with the provider to handle your request where we are able to identify your anonymous identifier.

If we make material changes, we will update the Effective Date above and, where appropriate, notify you within the app. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
